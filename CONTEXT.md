# Portfolio site

Humberto's professional site at hbfelizzola.de: a central place that presents his career as a software developer, aimed first at freelance clients and second at recruiters.

## Language

### Career

**Experience**:
A role held over a period of time, either employment at an employer or a freelance engagement with a client. It is either current or previous.
_Avoid_: Job, position, work history entry

**Timeline**:
All Experiences in date order, the career at a glance.
_Avoid_: Career path, CV, résumé

### Showcase

**Project**:
A specific thing Humberto built, shown with what it is, his role, and the tech involved. It may belong to one Experience, or stand alone as a personal project.
_Avoid_: Case study, work sample, portfolio item

**Portfolio**:
The collection of all Projects on the site.
_Avoid_: Showcase, gallery

### Audience

**Client**:
A person or company that hires Humberto for freelance work, past, current or prospective. Prospective Clients are the site's primary audience.
_Avoid_: Customer, lead

**Recruiter**:
A hiring manager or recruiter evaluating Humberto for employment, the site's secondary audience.
_Avoid_: Employer, HR
